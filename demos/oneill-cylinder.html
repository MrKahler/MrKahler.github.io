About the physics

Spin rate is set so the floor gives the chosen gravity: ω = √(g/R). Gravity falls off linearly toward the axis (g ∝ r), so it is zero at the centerline.

The ball is integrated in the habitat's rotating frame with centrifugal (ω²r), Coriolis (−2ω×v) and optional quadratic drag. Because the air rotates with the habitat, drag acts on velocity relative to the floor.

Throw spinward (the direction the floor moves) and the ball gets "heavier" and lands short; throw antispinward and it floats long. In the inertial frame the ball simply flies straight while the floor turns beneath it.

Day/night is simplified: the long mirrors hinge at the far end, and their tilt sets how much reflected sunlight enters the windows. Fully closed means night.
