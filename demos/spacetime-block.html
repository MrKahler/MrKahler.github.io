Look

Drag to turn. Right-drag or Shift-drag to move in all three directions. Scroll to zoom. On a touchscreen, one finger turns and two fingers move and zoom.

Reading the block

  • Left and right are x, up and down are y, and depth is time. Each slice is one frame of the video.
  • Something that stays put for a while draws a line straight along the time axis.
  • Steady motion draws a straight, slanted line. The faster it moves, the further the line leans away from the time axis.
  • Speeding up bends the line into a curve. Circling makes a spiral.
  • Pixels that barely change count as scenery. Lower the scenery opacity to fade them out and leave only the worldlines of moving things.
